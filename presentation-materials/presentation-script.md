# Smart Lost & Found Portal — Presentation Script (8–10 Minutes)

## Slide 1 — Title (30 seconds)
Good morning everyone. My project is called Smart Lost & Found Portal. It is a web application made for a college campus. The main purpose is to help students report lost items and return found items in a simple and organized way.

## Slide 2 — Introduction (45 seconds)
In every college, students sometimes lose important things like ID cards, books, keys, earphones, and water bottles. Usually, they ask friends or post messages in groups. This process is slow and unorganized. Our portal provides one central place where students can report and search for items.

## Slide 3 — Problem Statement (50 seconds)
The current manual process has three main problems. First, notices can be missed. Second, there is no proper record of lost and found items. Third, a person may wrongly claim an item. This project solves these issues through searchable reports and administrator approval.

## Slide 4 — Objectives (45 seconds)
The objectives are to make reporting quick, make searching easy, maintain a proper record, and make claims safer. The application should also be easy for students to use without technical training.

## Slide 5 — Technology Stack (60 seconds)
The frontend is built with Streamlit and custom CSS. Python is used as the application backend. SQLite stores users, item reports, and claims. This stack is simple, lightweight, and suitable for a college project. The app can be deployed using Streamlit Community Cloud.

## Slide 6 — Modules (75 seconds)
There are six main pages. Home introduces the project and shows statistics. Login controls access. Dashboard gives a quick overview. Report Lost Item lets users add details and images. Browse Items provides search and filters. Admin Panel lets an administrator delete fake reports and approve valid claims.

## Slide 7 — Screenshots (60 seconds)
Here we can see the blue dashboard interface and the browse page. The cards show item type, category, location, date, and status. This design is clean and responsive, so users can understand it quickly.

## Slide 8 — Demo Flow (75 seconds)
In the demo, a student logs in and submits a lost or found item report. Another student searches the available reports and submits a claim with a reason. Finally, the administrator reviews the pending claim. If it is genuine, the administrator approves it and the item status becomes claimed.

## Slide 9 — Future Scope (60 seconds)
In the future, this project can include email notifications, matching suggestions based on keywords, QR codes, user registration, and a cloud database for permanent storage. It can also be extended for multiple campuses.

## Slide 10 — Thank You (25 seconds)
To conclude, Smart Lost & Found Portal turns a manual campus problem into a simple digital process. It saves time, improves transparency, and helps students recover their belongings. Thank you. I am ready for your questions.
